Synthetic recovery

Compare direct measurement, prediction of fitted-model perturbations, and complete generator recovery across 60 cohorts.

Recovery criteria

Perturbation prediction passes with sign accuracy ≥ 0.90 and R² ≥ 0.80. Complete recovery also requires the selected dimension and Jacobian rank to be within one of the true dimension, and the output-influence overlap to be ≥ 0.80. The exact-system counts come from the archived aggregate table.
